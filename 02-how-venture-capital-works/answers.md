# Lesson 2 — LPs & GPs

## Why do LPs stay out of investment decisions?

### My Answer

LPs trust that GPs have the expertise, experience, and team to allocate capital effectively, just as founders are trusted to build companies.

---

### AI Feedback

Excellent.

LPs delegate investment decisions because specialization creates better outcomes.

---

## If Harvard gives Sequoia $100M...

### My Answer

The LP ultimately bears the financial loss.

That is part of venture capital's risk profile.

---

### AI Feedback

Correct.

The GP risks reputation and future fundraising.

The LP risks capital.
