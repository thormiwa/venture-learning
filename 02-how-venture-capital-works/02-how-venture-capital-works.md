# 1. Why Venture Capital Exists

Most startups cannot obtain bank loans because they lack:

- Predictable revenue
- Assets that can be used as collateral
- Stable cash flow

Banks optimize for certainty.

Venture capitalists optimize for asymmetric upside.

A VC expects many companies to fail if a few generate extraordinary returns.

---

## Bank vs Venture Capital

### Bank

The bank expects:

- Regular repayments
- Interest
- Low risk

The bank does not benefit significantly if the company becomes enormous.

---

### Venture Capital

The VC expects:

- No guaranteed repayment
- High uncertainty
- Ownership in the company

If the company succeeds, everyone benefits.

---

## Debt vs Equity

### Debt

The company borrows money.

The money must be repaid.

The lender does not become an owner.

Examples:

- Bank loans
- Venture debt
- Credit facilities

---

### Equity

The investor buys ownership in the company.

There is no repayment schedule.

If the company fails, the investor may lose everything.

If the company succeeds, both founder and investor win together.

---

## When Debt Makes Sense

Debt works well when a company has:

- Predictable cash flow
- Existing customers
- Assets
- Confidence it can repay

Examples:

- Apple
- Walmart
- Local supermarket

---

## When Equity Makes Sense

Equity is better when:

- Revenue is uncertain
- Growth matters more than profits
- The company needs capital to build

Examples:

- Stripe (early years)
- Airbnb
- OpenAI (early)
- SpaceX (early)

# 2. Venture Capital Stakeholders

Every venture fund has two major groups.

## Limited Partners (LPs)

LPs provide the money.

Examples:

- University endowments
- Pension funds
- Foundations
- Family offices
- Sovereign wealth funds

LPs invest in funds.

They do not choose startups.

---

## General Partners (GPs)

GPs manage the fund.

Responsibilities include:

- Finding startups
- Evaluating founders
- Making investments
- Helping portfolio companies
- Raising future funds
- Returning capital to LPs

The GP is a professional capital allocator.

---

## Why LPs Stay Out of Investment Decisions

LPs invest because they trust the GP's judgment.

Just as founders build companies,

GPs build investment portfolios.

---

# 3. Why Relationships Matter

Great startups often don't need money immediately.

Great VCs build relationships years before investing.

VCs compete for founders.

Money alone is rarely enough.

Great firms provide:

- Hiring
- Recruiting
- Customer introductions
- Strategic advice
- Network
- Brand

---

# 4. Venture Funds

A venture fund is a pool of capital.

Example:

LPs commit:

Harvard .......... $40M

Yale .............. $30M

Family Office ..... $30M

-------------------------

Total Fund ........ $100M

The GP manages the entire fund.

# 5. Fund Economics

A VC firm earns money in two ways.

## Management Fees

Purpose:

Operate the investment firm.

Typical:

2% annually.

Pays for:

- Salaries
- Office
- Research
- Travel
- Legal
- Operations

Example:

Fund size = $200M

Annual fee = 2%

Management fee:

$4M per year

Management fees are paid whether investments succeed or fail.

---

## Carry (Carried Interest)

Carry is performance compensation.

Typical:

20%

Carry only begins after LPs receive back all of their committed capital.

---

## Capital Return Hurdle

Example:

Fund Size:

$100M

Exit 1:

+$30M

All goes to LPs.

GP receives:

$0

Exit 2:

+$70M

LPs have now recovered $100M.

GP still receives:

$0

Exit 3:

+$50M

This is now profit.

Split:

LPs:

80%

GP:

20%

Carry has officially begun.

---

## Why Carry Exists

Without a hurdle:

GPs could become wealthy while LPs lose money.

Carry aligns incentives.

The GP only earns significant performance compensation after creating value for LPs.

---

## Management Fees vs Carry

| Management Fee | Carry |
|----------------|-------|
| Annual | Only after profits |
| Keeps firm operating | Rewards investment success |
| Paid regardless of performance | Only paid after LP capital is returned |
| Predictable income | Performance bonus |

---

# Key Mental Models

Banks optimize for certainty.

VCs optimize for upside.

Equity aligns incentives.

Money is only one product a VC sells.

Great founders choose investors.

Great investors choose founders.

The GP's job is not simply to invest.

The GP's job is to compound LP capital over decades.

Carry rewards performance.

Management fees keep the lights on.

Always reason from incentives.
